Radar & Provider Account

Closes the precipitation map after inactivity to limit accidental extended viewing. The timer resets when you interact with the map. No service restart is needed.

RainViewer supplies up to 2 hours of past radar frames, refreshed about every 10 minutes. It is free for personal or educational use; no account or key is required. Forecast frames are provided by Rainbow Weather.

Rainbow's monthly free allowance is 30,000 map tiles and 5,000 point-nowcast requests. The radar map uses the tile allowance; it does not call the point-nowcast API. After the free allowance, published rates are $0.20 per 1,000 tiles and $0.10 per 1,000 nowcast calls.

Create your own Rainbow developer account to use the paid, pay-as-you-go option. Paid usage is disabled by default; this device stops requesting tiles when its local estimate reaches the 30,000 free allowance. Rainbow shares usage across the account, so this is not an account-wide spending cap. Check the provider dashboard before enabling paid usage.
